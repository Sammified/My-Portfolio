01 41.5M+ impressions in about three months Two founders on XWeb3 media · Anonymized

The problem

Two founders needed to grow on X at the same time, in a space where attention moves fast and a quiet week costs you ground.

What I did

I ghostwrote for both and ran their accounts. A monthly content bank kept both feeds consistent, and a daily reply strategy put them in front of the right conversations, so each post built on the last.

The result

21.7M impressions on one account and 19.8M on the other.

21.7M, first founder19.8M, second founder
02 0.9% → 6.3% trial-to-paid in 11 weeks B2B AI SaaSAI / SaaS · Anonymized

The problem

Trial-to-paid sat at 0.9%. The founder wanted to redesign the pricing page. I told him to wait. The landing page was speaking to everyone in the category, so the people the product served best couldn't see it was for them.

What I did

Two weeks of conversations with users who paid, stayed and referred others. Then we rewrote the headline, hero, onboarding emails and in-app tooltips around the one person the product served best. No new features. No redesign. No paid ads.

The result

Trial-to-paid went from 0.9% to 6.3% in 11 weeks.

What I do

04 29.3K views and 3 inbound leads in 30 days Murals.ngArt & design · Named

The problem

Murals.ng runs several business lines: Spaces, House of Eweka, Warlocks and Mage Forge. The work was excellent. How clearly the market understood each line wasn't.

What I did

As Head of Brand & Communications, I started with clarity, not content: what Murals.ng is, who it speaks to, what makes it different. Then I carried those answers across the website, Instagram and the way the team talks about the work.

The result

29.3K+ views and 3 inbound leads in the last 30 days of my first month. July to October 2026.
